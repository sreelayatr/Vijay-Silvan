My Services & Offerings

Each package is designed around your celebration, providing coverage for every moment, guidance throughout the day, and an elegant gallery of images of your wedding. Whether it’s an intimate elopement, a full-day celebration, or a portrait session, my goal is to make sure every detail, connection, and memory is preserved beautifully.

The way you glance at one another, the gentle touch of your hands, the veil catching the wind and making you laugh. These are the moments you’ll want to hold onto, not just how they looked, but how they felt, the little details that make your day unmistakably yours.

Packages

Weddings

Full-day coverage capturing your entire celebration, from getting ready to the last dance. I photograph the moments and details that make your day yours, including family and group shots, candid interactions, and editorial-style portraits. You’ll receive a curated gallery of fully edited high-resolution images, delivered online for sharing and printing.

from: $3750

Elopements

Intimate, often adventurous celebrations, captured with intention. I focus on your connection, the setting, and small details like vows, rings, and meaningful gestures. Includes guidance on timing and locations, as well as a curated gallery of fully edited images that let you relive the day for years to come.

from: $2500

Portraits

Engagement, personal, or lifestyle sessions tailored to your vision. I create relaxed, editorial-style images that highlight your personality, connection, and style. Sessions include pre-shoot guidance and a curated online gallery of fully edited images ready for sharing or print.

from: $1500
