Wedding photographer

I am Vijay Silvan

I’m a wedding photographer based in New York City, and I travel for destination weddings worldwide. My approach combines editorial precision with a focus on real, unscripted moments, capturing weddings that feel stylish and authentic. As if browsing a fashion magazine or stepping into a museum. Outside of photography, I find inspiration wandering city streets, reading historical fiction, getting lost in old films, or spending hours in art galleries.

My philosophy

I believe in thoughtful, considered, and personal collaborations

I like to let the couple guide the story, shaping my approach around who you are. I want to hear your story, how you met, and about the people who matter most in your lives. Weddings are once-in-a-lifetime celebrations, and every detail, from the venue and flowers to the veil and makeup, deserves thoughtful attention. I photograph with care and intention, because these images are more than pictures. They are memories. I hope that when you look back at them, you feel both the emotion of the day and the beauty of each moment.
